How I lead

From QA Engineer to QA Manager at QA Wolf in three years, now running quality across multiple product teams.

Today

  • Lead 3 QA engineering teams, each with a QA Lead reporting to me
  • Manage 3 floating QA engineers and an automation engineer who cover cross-team priorities
  • Set test automation strategy and quality standards, including scalable Playwright and JavaScript automation
  • Work across Product Engineering, Customer Success, Sales and QA leadership to match quality work to business priorities
  • Mentor QA Leads and engineers on technical growth and leadership

Where it comes from

As a field organizer, I managed five teams of organizers in training and coached more than 100 worker leaders at LaGuardia, Reagan National and George Mason University. As a union representative for San Francisco city and county workers, I interpreted contracts and policy and bargained for better wages.

That work taught me to earn trust with people who don't share my vocabulary and to keep many parties moving toward one deadline. QA leadership turns out to need the same things.
